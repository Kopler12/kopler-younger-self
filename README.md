Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76) ($2).

`main.py` writes a single line — the one line I would send back to my younger self — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> "Don't waste your best years ironing your shirt for people who won't remember your name — the only crease that counts is the one you get standing up for the kid nobody picks."

The line is addressed straight at a younger self: stop dressing for an audience that will forget you, and keep the mark you earn standing up for someone smaller. One line, 173 characters, no setup paragraph needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
